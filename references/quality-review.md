# Quality Review

## Candidate QA

Check:

- device identity;
- critical component count and placement;
- proportions;
- interfaces/connections;
- supports;
- unexplained AI inventions;
- candidate consistency within one style batch.

## Line/Vector QA

Check:

- complete silhouette;
- regular circles/flanges;
- straight support/pipe edges;
- repeated fastener consistency;
- lack of thick-paint or doubled borders;
- real vector output, not embedded raster.

## Coloring QA

Check separately from line geometry:

- LINEART geometry unchanged;
- base colors match intended style/reference;
- no region-fill spill;
- shadows/highlights remain restrained;
- glass treatment does not obscure internal structure.

## Export QA

### Editable Master

Verify AI reopens, layers remain editable, and geometry-lock evidence still passes.

### Presentation SVG

Verify through actual PowerPoint insertion when PPT compatibility is required. Inspect:

- complete canvas;
- color visibility;
- component connections;
- white seams;
- missing outlines;
- unexpected compositing changes.

Do not declare PPT compatibility from browser/Illustrator rendering alone.
